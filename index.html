Demos

  • Demo 1

    Carnival Ride: Sky Swinger

    A 150 m tall "flying swings" tower ride (a star-flyer style ride). You walk up to the ride, pick one of twelve swing seats, and ride it. The collar and star lift 125 m up the tower, the star spins up to 90°/s, the seats fan out under the spin, and everything comes back down and stops. You can watch from the ground or ride in a seat.

    Launch Demo

  • Demo 2

    Sports Trainer: Basketball Free Throw

    A basketball shooting trainer in a gym. You pick up the ball and throw it with your real arm motion. The ball flies with real mass, size, air drag, spin and bounce. Every shot is measured and graded: release speed, angle, backspin, where it crossed the rim and how steeply. A scoreboard over the hoop gives a coaching tip for the next shot.

    Launch Demo

  • Demo 3

    Procedural Trainer: Lockout/Tagout

    A box is jammed in a running conveyor. Before you reach in, you have to lock the machine out by following the OSHA energy-control sequence: stop the machine, isolate the power, apply your lock, hang your tag, verify zero voltage, try to start it, and only then clear the jam. The trainer rejects steps done out of order, counts each mistake, and finishes with your time and mistake count.

    Launch Demo

  • Demo 4

    Scavenger Hunt: Office Tower at Night

    You're on floors 20–22 of a 45-storey glass office tower at night, with the city lit up outside. Five office objects are on your list, hidden among twenty look-alike props across three themed floors joined by a stairwell. Each grab tells you whether it was a target or a decoy. The clock stops when you find the fifth.

    Launch Demo
